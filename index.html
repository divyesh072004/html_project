Solopreneur Launch Checklist

Interactive 5-step roadmap to turn business ideas into operational startups. Check tasks as you achieve them. Your progress persists in real time.

Operational Milestones
1. Define & Narrow Your Niche Target Pinpoint exactly who has the highest financial pain point. Interview 3 prospects before building.
2. Set Up Professional Email & Landing Page Secure a clean domain. Build a single-page landing site emphasizing value proposition, with an intake form.
3. Structure Your Premium MVP Offer Design a concrete package (what they get, timelines, and outcomes) rather than billing hourly rates.
4. Deploy High-Volume Cold Outreach Campaigns Reach out to 25 target leads daily via personalized channels (LinkedIn, Cold email) offering quick leverage reviews.
5. Deliver and Secure Long-Term Retainers Overdeliver for early clients to secure proof of results, testimonies, and transition them into Monthly Retainers.

Solopreneur Rules for Rapid Launch

  • 1. Sell first, build later. Do not write complex lines of code or buy stock until you secure cash commitments or active pre-orders.
  • 2. Price for profit margins. Avoid high capital/low margin platforms. Target 75%+ margins so a few customers sustain you.
  • 3. Productize your output. Deliver standardized tasks/projects. It prevents scope creep and allows scaling with freelancers.
